# 21Vianet (VNET Group, Inc.)

> 21Vianet is the largest carrier-neutral internet and data center services provider in China, operating 50+ data centers across 20+ cities. It provides hosting, managed hosting, interconnectivity, cloud, and hybrid IT infrastructure to enterprise customers, and is the exclusive operator of Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform in mainland China through its Blue Cloud subsidiary. Nasdaq: VNET.
